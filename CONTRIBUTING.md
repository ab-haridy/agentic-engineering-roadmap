# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Agentic Engineering Roadmap. This repository holds the roadmap itself. Your own work on it belongs in your copy, made with **Use this template**, not here.

## Ways to help

### Report a problem

Found a broken link, an unclear task, or a Definition of Done that can't be checked? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ab-haridy/agentic-engineering-roadmap/issues/new) and say which module and what is wrong.

### Suggest a job description

Good practice JDs are the hardest part of the roadmap to find. To suggest one, open an issue with:

- **The full text** of a real job description posted publicly, for example on a freelance marketplace.
- **Names and identifying details removed:** the client, the company, people, links, and anything that points to them, such as a niche product or a location.
- **The field** (Web / SaaS, Mobile or CMS) and why it suits the roadmap: users, data, and a scope that can be cut to an MVP.

If the client has applied anything hidden to filter applicants, such as "start your proposal with the word…", leave it out.

### Improve a module

For a small fix, such as a typo, a broken link or a clearer sentence, open a pull request directly.

For a bigger change, such as a new task, a different deliverable or a new resource, open an issue first so we can agree on it before you write it.

When you edit a module, keep its structure: Objective, Why it matters, Practical task, Deliverable, Definition of Done, Resources, Next. Every item in a Definition of Done should be something a reviewer can check.

## What not to send

- Your deliverables for a module: they go in the `work/` folder of your own copy.
- Paid or copyrighted material you don't have the right to share.
- Job descriptions with identifying details still in them.
