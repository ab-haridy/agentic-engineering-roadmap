## About the project

We are an AI solutions company seeking an experienced full-stack developer to help build an innovative AI-powered restaurant management platform.

Our vision is to develop an intelligent, data-driven solution that helps restaurants, restaurant chains and food service operators optimize daily operations, reduce food waste, improve purchasing decisions, automate administrative processes and increase profitability.

We are particularly interested in applying artificial intelligence to meal demand forecasting, inventory optimization, procurement, production planning and financial performance analysis.

We are looking for a resourceful developer who can help translate this vision into a functional, scalable SaaS product while recommending efficient, cost-effective development approaches.

## Key features and functionality

The following describes our long-term product vision. We understand that development will be completed in phases and do not expect every feature to be included in the initial MVP.

### 1. AI-powered meal demand forecasting

- Forecast meal demand based on historical sales and consumption data.
- Analyze demand patterns by restaurant, branch, menu category and day.
- Incorporate relevant factors such as local events, holidays, seasons and historical trends.
- Generate recommended production quantities.
- Help operators reduce overproduction, shortages and food waste.

### 2. Intelligent inventory management

- Track ingredient and product inventory.
- Monitor stock levels, usage and expiry dates.
- Generate low-stock alerts and replenishment recommendations.
- Analyze ingredient consumption against meal production.
- Identify potential inventory inefficiencies and waste.

### 3. AI procurement and supplier management

- Maintain supplier profiles and purchasing records.
- Compare supplier quotations and ingredient pricing.
- Generate suggested purchase orders based on inventory and forecasted demand.
- Analyze supplier costs and purchasing trends.
- Identify opportunities for purchasing optimization.

### 4. Meal production and menu planning

- Manage menus, recipes and ingredient requirements.
- Calculate ingredient quantities based on planned meal production.
- Generate daily and weekly production plans.
- Estimate recipe costs and cost per meal.
- Assist with menu optimization based on costs and demand.

### 5. Financial intelligence and profitability analytics

- Track food costs, operating expenses and meal profitability.
- Analyze financial performance by restaurant, branch or location.
- Compare budgeted versus actual costs.
- Generate dashboards showing revenue, costs, margins and operational KPIs.
- Identify potential cost-saving opportunities using AI-assisted analysis.

### 6. AI document processing and administrative automation

- Import data from Excel spreadsheets, CSV files and existing business systems.
- Extract relevant information from supplier invoices and purchasing documents.
- Automate recurring reports and data entry where feasible.
- Reduce manual spreadsheet work and repetitive administrative processes.

### 7. AI operations assistant

An integrated AI assistant capable of helping operators:

- Analyze operational and financial data.
- Explain trends in food costs, waste, inventory and purchasing.
- Generate management reports and operational summaries.
- Identify unusual spending or consumption patterns.
- Suggest purchasing and production adjustments.
- Answer questions using authorized company data.

### 8. Multi-location management

- Centralized dashboard for operators managing multiple restaurants or branches.
- Location-specific inventory, production, purchasing and financial reporting.
- Consolidated management reporting.
- Role-based access for management, finance, procurement and operational teams.

## Development approach

We are looking to build the platform in stages, starting with a focused minimum viable product (MVP). For the initial release, we would like to prioritize:

- A clean, modern dashboard for restaurant operations.
- Excel/CSV data import.
- Basic meal demand forecasting using historical data.
- Inventory and purchasing visibility.
- Cost and profitability reporting.
- AI-assisted operational insights.

We are open to developer recommendations on how to simplify and prioritize these capabilities while maintaining a strong foundation for future development.

The platform should initially be a responsive web application. Native mobile apps are not required at this stage.
