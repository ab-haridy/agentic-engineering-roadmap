# Mobile App Developer (iOS & Android): custom branded client app

**Field:** Mobile · **Size:** Medium · **Good for:** working against a third-party API, store publishing, family accounts

---

## Summary

We are looking for an experienced Mobile App Developer to design and build a branded client-facing mobile application for our children's sports school, available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Our backend systems are managed on a third-party booking platform. The app will act as a customized front end, using that platform for scheduling, bookings, payments, user accounts and memberships.

## Requirements and features

1. **Branding and store publishing**
   - Custom app name, logo, app icon and brand colors tailored to our brand.
   - Full assistance with submitting and publishing on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

2. **Data from the booking platform.** Fetch schedules, bookings, payments and account data seamlessly through the platform's APIs.

3. **Custom customer experience and account flow**
   - Streamlined onboarding and account registration flow.
   - Intuitive multi-profile / dependent account management.

Please share your relevant experience in mobile app development.

---

**Practice note:** you won't have access to the client's booking platform. Define the API you expect it to have and build against a mock of it, or use a booking platform that offers free developer access. Write down which one you chose and why.
