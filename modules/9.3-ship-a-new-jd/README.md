# 9.3 · Run a new job description end to end and ship

**Phase 9 · Capstone: the AI software factory** · Estimated time: 40–60 hours

## Objective

Prove the factory by delivering a new project with it, from a raw job description to a deployed MVP and a clean client handover, rather than by demonstrating agents on their own.

## Why it matters

A factory is only proven when it delivers something it has never seen. This is the closest the roadmap gets to real client work: an unfamiliar request, your full process, and a result a client could accept.

## Practical task

1. **Choose a new job description**: one you have **not** used anywhere in this roadmap. Find it on a freelance marketplace, in your field or in a second field your factory supports.
2. **Run it through the whole factory.** Make every human decision yourself at the gates, and record each one.
3. **Ship the MVP**, deployed or runnable on demand.
4. **Prepare the client handover** with the clean project only. Your factory's internal material (instructions, prompts, skills, orchestration, agent logs) is your intellectual property. Leave it out unless a contract requires it.
5. **Write a retrospective.** What worked, what broke, what took longer than it should have, and what you will change in the factory. Make at least the most important of those changes.
6. **Prove it is reusable.** Show that the factory can start a second job description right away, without redesign: take one through intake to an approved brief.

## Deliverable

The client-facing package:

- Product brief
- Architecture
- Project backlog
- Working MVP
- Test and eval evidence
- Security review
- Deployment
- README and runbook
- Feature and specification documentation
- Client handover package

And for you:

- A retrospective, with the improvements made to the factory
- The second job description's intake, as far as an approved brief

```
work/9.3-ship-a-new-jd/
├── README.md            # the project, links to the client package and the live MVP
├── decisions.md         # every human decision at the gates
├── retrospective.md     # what worked, what broke, what changed in the factory
└── second-jd.md         # the second job description through intake to an approved brief
```

## Definition of Done

- [ ] A new job description was processed end to end
- [ ] The MVP is deployed or runnable on demand
- [ ] The acceptance criteria are met
- [ ] The quality and security gates passed
- [ ] The client handover is clean and complete
- [ ] The factory's internal material is kept out of the deliverable
- [ ] The retrospective identifies improvements, and the most important ones are made
- [ ] The factory took a second job description to an approved brief without redesign
